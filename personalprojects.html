Personal Projects

Welcome to my creative showcase—a space where my ideas take shape through a variety of mediums. Here you’ll find hand-drawn sketches, colored-pencil illustrations to mixed-media experiments. Most of my personal projects have to do with, well, my personal life. Whether it’s drawing pets, childhood memories, memorials, or random experiments, each finished piece reflects my passion for hands-on creation.

Hydrosphere Sciences Logo

This is a logo system and brand guide I developed for Hydrosphere Sciences, who was looking for a rebrand from their previous company name. I created a primary logo along with variants and a brand use guide. I used Adobe Illustrator for the logo development and InDesign to put the brand guide together.

Stephen

I drew this in honor of my family member who passed away. I created this using colored pencil and graphite on bristol board.

Bandit

The adult version of Baby Bandit. I created this using colored pencil on mixed media paper.

Alien

This is an experimental piece I did exploring the uses of a gelli plate for printmaking. I used acrylic paint and newspaper on mixed media paper with the gelli plate to create this.

Angel

This is a drawing of my grandparents' friend's late Aunt Naomi. When she passed away, my grandparents asked if I could do this as a memorial gift for the family. I drew this on bristol board using graphite and colored pencil.

Baby Bandit

This drawing is of my dog when he was a puppy, hence the title "Baby Bandit." I used colored pencil on Bristol board to complete this.

Pookie

This is a colored pencil drawing on bristol board of my grandmother's cat, Pookie, who has crossed the rainbow bridge. I gave this to my grandparents as a Christmas gift.

Skylar

This colored pencil drawing was a gift for my Great-Aunt Sue. Skylar is her cat who crossed the rainbow bridge. This was done using colored pencil on bristol board.

Me

I drew this during my senior year of high school, reflecting on my younger self. This is a photo of me as a child, and I submitted it into the Katonah Museum of Art's Senior Exhibition. I used colored pencils on Bristol board.

St. Joseph's

This is a chalk pastel piece of my childhood church, St. Joseph's. It has since been knocked down, but before it did I wanted to make something to memorialize it. I grew up getting my sacraments here, so it was a special and nostalgic place for me.

Never Trust a Happy Song

This is a recreation of Grouplove's album "Never Trust A Happy Song" using acrylic paint.
