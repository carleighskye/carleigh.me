About Me

Hi, I'm Carleigh! I am a UX/UI Designer and Web Developer who is passionate about creating meaningful digital experiences.

I am currently a junior studying Media Arts and Design at James Madison University, but I am originally based out of the New York Metropolitan Area. At JMU, I also hold a concentration in Interactive Design as well as a minor in Educational Media. I am very passionate about design and creating accessible technology.

My design approach is simple-allow the user to achieve their goals in as few clicks as possible. I like to think of this as the golf method of design. ;)

I am skilled in Figma prototyping, as well as Sublime Text, Adobe Suite, Github, and Canva. Currently, I am also proficient in HTML, CSS, C, and Bootstrap. This summer, I am taking a Python course to further my skill set and learn new ways of web development.

Outside of the classroom, you will probably find me listening to music. Some of my favorite bands are Fountains of Wayne, Electric Light Orchestra, and The Format. If I'm doing anything else, I'm either drawing, exercising, or hanging out with my cat, Phoebe.

Thank you for taking the time to check out my portfolio, I look forward to connecting with you! :)
