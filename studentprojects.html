Student Work

Welcome to my student work! A dynamic collection of projects born from my time at JMU. Here you'll find everything from design prototypes, coding exercises, brand guide development, exhibit creation, and more. Each piece reflects the skills and concepts I've explored in the classroom—whether that's honing critical thinking through written analysis, experimenting with new media and softwares within assignments, or collaborating on group projects. Dive in to see how these academic challenges became hands‑on projects, and see how each exploration guides me to a better understanding of my field, and of my own creative growth.
