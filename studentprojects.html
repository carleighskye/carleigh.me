Student Work

Welcome to my student work! A dynamic collection of projects born from my time at JMU. Here you'll find everything from design prototypes, coding exercises, brand guide development, exhibit creation, and more. Each piece reflects the skills and concepts I've explored in the classroom—whether that's honing critical thinking through written analysis, experimenting with new media and softwares within assignments, or collaborating on group projects. Dive in to see how these academic challenges became hands‑on projects, and see how each exploration guides me to a better understanding of my field, and of my own creative growth.

Global Career Accelerator

I completed the Web Development for Everyone track through the Global Career Accelerator, a hands-on program focused on building foundational web development skills. Throughout the course, I worked with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript to design and build functional, user-friendly websites and interactive web features. The class emphasized real-world application through project-based learning, including using modern tools and AI to support the development process. I applied basic UI/UX principles, problem-solving skills, and industry workflows to create projects that demonstrate both technical ability and thoughtful design. In addition to technical skills, the program supported professional growth through collaboration, feedback, and exposure to industry practices, helping prepare me for real-world web development and digital roles.
