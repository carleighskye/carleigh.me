Social Media Info Post Series

Project Overview

I designed an educational infographic focused on cybersecurity, aimed at audiences with little prior knowledge of the topic. The goal was to communicate why cybersecurity matters using clear, approachable, and visually engaging information design.

UX/UI Approach

Applying UX/UI principles, I prioritized clarity, visual hierarchy, and scannability through the use of icons, concise text, and structured layout. Neon green accents and technology-inspired details were used to create emphasis and reinforce the digital theme, while typography choices conveyed urgency and importance.

My Abilities

This project demonstrates my ability to research complex topics, translate information into accessible visuals, and design user-centered educational content using InDesign.
