Grow With the Flow App Development

Project Overview

The goal of this team project was to create a high-fidelity app prototype over the course of a semester. “Grow with the Flow” is a mental health application geared to promote social activity and self care to users, especially with sensitivity to the needs of multiple age ranges and those who may have an attention deficit disorder. Our app aims to easily locate quality social and educational resources at any age. The prototype was created using Figma and Miro was used to consolidate user research and findings.

Video Walkthroughs

Task 1

User wants to explore resources for online and in-person therapy that is personalized and location-based.

Task 2

User wishes to identify and track daily moods and trends, and view past entries.

Task 3

User wishes to learn about and schedule social activities in their area with others.

Task 4

User wishes to relieve nerves and decompress through use of in-app mini games.
