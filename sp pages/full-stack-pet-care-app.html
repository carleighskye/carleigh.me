Full-Stack Pet Care App

Project Overview

The goal of this app was to create an app to help brand new pet owners learn how to care for their animals. This app shows tips on how to care for different kinds of pets, ranging from cats/dogs to reptiles, and more. This app is not trying to replace veterinary care, but rather help people who are completely new to caring for pets. I wanted to make learning about different animals easier for people who have pets, looking to adopt a pet, or just wanting to learn more about pet care.
