CSEMSC Brand Guide

Project Overview

For this project, my class was tasked to create a new logo and brand guide for Central Shenandoah EMS Council. CSEMSC representatives came to our class to discuss who they are and what their brand represents. We used the information gathered to direct our initial designs. I used Adobe Illustrator to develop the new logo and typeface, and Adobe InDesign to put together the brand guide.

Project Development

To develop this project, I completed a creative brief outlining the project and its goals. I also gathered visual inspiration as well as developed thumbnails, notes, and color exploration to help further the design process.
