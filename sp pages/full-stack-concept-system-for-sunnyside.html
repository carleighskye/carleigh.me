Full-Stack Concept System for Sunnyside

Project Overview

The title of this app is Nature Companion. The purpose of this project was to create an interactive app for residents of Sunnyside Communities to use when on their trails or exploring the grounds. This product is meant to foster curiosity and a place for residents to learn more about the nearby wildlife, as well as connect with other residents. This app is solving the problem of users not knowing what kinds of plants or animals they are looking at when on the trails or Sunnyside grounds.
