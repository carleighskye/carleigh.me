Glenwood Caverns

Project Overview

For this project, my class was tasked to pick a project prompt from “The Real Show 2025”. My group chose to redesign Glenwood Cavern’s website. We wanted to go for a vintage western style, sticking to their original color palette. We redesigned the home page, the thrill rides page, and the defiance coaster page. I primarily worked on the thrill rides page, and developed the stamp assets using adobe illustrator. We put together the mockup site on Figma to visualize what it would look like on a real screen.
