charity: water game

Project Overview

My task from the marketing team at charity: water was to develop a game inspired by their global mission of providing clean water for all. The goal of the game being to raise awareness among young people. Using HTML, CSS, and Javascript, I built “Water Drop”. In this game, users collect as many falling water drops as possible while avoiding pollutants (bad drops). Users tap to collect drops before they hit the ground. I began with focusing on core functionality, with clickable elements, score tracking, a timer, and a basic layout of the game. As I continued to develop the game, I added difficulty modes, and additional small features such as confetti when a user reaches a new high score, and score penalties for each pollutant pressed. I wanted to make this game fun and engaging for all users, while also portraying charity: water’s message and brand throughout the game. Open the game in your browser for best viewing.
