CVS Logo Redesign & Brand Guide

Project Overview

For this project, I was tasked to pick one organization and give them a redesigned logo and brand guide. I chose CVS Pharmacy for this redesign. I wanted to stick to their main color scheme of red and white, but I wanted to give their logo a better representation of what it is that CVS does and represents. I used Adobe Illustrator and Canva for this redesign.
