Hydrosphere Sciences Logo & Branding

Project Overview

This project consisted of developing a new brand guide for a company rebrand. Hydrosphere Sciences is a team of geoscientists and engineers who solve complex water and environmental challenges. Previously known as Mutch Associates, the company wanted to keep some aspects of their original logo within the new Hydrosphere Sciences Logo. I developed the logo and assets in Adobe Illustrator and put together the brand guide in Adobe InDesign.
