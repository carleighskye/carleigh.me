Informative Infographic

Project Overview

I designed a data-driven infographic focused on technology use in education, translating academic research and statistics into clear, engaging visuals. The goal was to make complex information easy to understand at a glance while maintaining accuracy and credibility.

Using UX/UI and visual design principles, I structured the content into clear sections, applied visual hierarchy, and used color, layout, and data visualizations to guide the viewer through the story. All visuals were intentionally designed to accurately represent real data and support comprehension.

This project demonstrates my ability to research, synthesize information, and design user-centered, visually compelling educational content.
