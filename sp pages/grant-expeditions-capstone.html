Grant Expeditions Capstone

Project Overview

This project consisted of redesigning and developing an existing website for Grant Expeditions tour company. Grant Expeditions is a locally owned business specializing in tours around Tanzania, Africa. My class partnered with the CIS program to build a fully functional product. This was a competition among 6 other talented teams, and our team ended up taking first place, and our solution is in the process of being implemented as their new website.

The primary goal of the Grant Expedition redesign is to deliver a modern, trustworthy, and streamlined end-to-end experience that supports both travelers and administrators.

To ensure the site works smoothly for all users, we made the entire platform fully responsive. Every page automatically adapts to different screen sizes, so travelers browsing on phones or tablets get the same smooth experience as desktop users.
